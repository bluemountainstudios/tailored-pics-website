<section class="bg-white/10 backdrop-blur-md rounded-3xl p-8 lg:p-12">
    <h1 class="text-4xl font-bold text-white mb-8">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
    
    <div class="space-y-6 text-left">
        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I create my first visual schedule?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Open <strong>My Schedules</strong> and tap the plus button. Add items, give the schedule a title, and tap save to create it.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I add or change images for items?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Tap an item’s image placeholder to open the image picker. You can search the built-in library or scroll through the full list. As you type an item’s title, Tailored Pics will also suggest an image automatically.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">Can I use my own photos?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Not yet. Right now, schedules use the built-in image library. If you’re missing something, go to <strong>Settings &gt; Feedback &gt; Suggest an Image</strong> and tell us what you need.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I check off items?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Enable check-off styles in a schedule’s <strong>Pro Settings</strong>, then tap items in the schedule to mark them complete. Styles include checkmark, star, gray out, strike-through, and hide.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I lock editing on a schedule?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Open a schedule, tap <strong>Options</strong>, and choose <strong>Lock Editing</strong>. Set your lock type in <strong>Settings &gt; Visual Schedules &gt; Editing Lock</strong> (PIN, Face ID/Touch ID, or a math challenge).</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How does Guided Access affect editing?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">If you use Guided Access, Tailored Pics can lock editing or lock the schedule view while Guided Access is active. Configure this in <strong>Settings &gt; Accessibility</strong>, then enable Guided Access in iOS Settings.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I print a visual schedule?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Print from the schedule list (swipe a schedule and tap Print) or from inside a schedule using <strong>Options &gt; Print</strong>. Printing requires Tailored Pics Pro and an AirPrint-compatible printer.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">Is my data synced across devices?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Not yet. Schedules are stored on your device, but you can move them using <strong>Settings &gt; Data Backup</strong> to export a <code>.tailoredPics</code> file and import it on another device.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">What’s included with Tailored Pics Pro?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Pro unlocks more than 3 schedules, printing, item position numbers, and multiple check-off styles.</p>
        </div>

        <div>
            <h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-3">How do I manage or cancel my subscription?</h3>
            <p class="text-white/80">Open <strong>Settings &gt; Subscription Center</strong> in the app, or manage your plan in iOS Settings &gt; Your Name &gt; Subscriptions.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
</section>
